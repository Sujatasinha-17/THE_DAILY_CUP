Since 2018

Started with one espresso machine and a folding table.

The Daily Cup began as a weekend cart outside a hardware store. What people kept coming back for wasn't just the espresso — it was the five minutes of quiet before the day got loud. That's still the whole idea. We roast close by, pour with care, and keep the room easy to sit in for longer than a coffee usually takes.

Today we're a full café with the same folding table still bolted to the wall by the door, as a reminder of where this started.
